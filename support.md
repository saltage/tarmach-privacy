# Tarmach support

For help with Tarmach, email [Tarmach support](mailto:tarmach.spill850@passmail.net). Include your iPhone model, iOS version and Tarmach version if you know them. A short description of what you tapped and what appeared helps diagnose the issue.

## Recording a journey

Start recording while parked. With **While Using** location access, a journey started in the app can continue with the screen locked. If you want a Shortcuts automation to start a journey when the app is closed, open **Automatic starts** in Settings for the setup steps.

## Adding a vehicle

Open **Garage** and tap **Add vehicle**. Enter the details you want to keep. Registration lookup is unavailable in the App Store version, and you can enter the vehicle details yourself.

## Exporting or deleting your data

To export journeys, open **Export and share** in Settings and choose GPX, CSV or GeoJSON. To clear your saved data, open **What leaves the phone** under **About** in Settings and choose **Delete all data**. This also removes raw motion captures saved by the app. Copies you have exported or shared are managed at their destination.
